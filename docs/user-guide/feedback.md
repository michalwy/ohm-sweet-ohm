# Sending feedback

**Send feedback** tells the people who build OhmSweetOhm about a problem, an idea or a question,
from wherever you are in the app.

## Where to find it

- In a workspace: **Send feedback** in the sidebar, under your email and the theme switch.
- On the **Workspaces** screen: the small **Send feedback** link at the bottom right.
- Not signed in: the small **Send feedback** link at the bottom of the **Sign in** and **Create
  account** pages, so you can report a problem with signing in too.

## Sending a report

Choose what it is — **A problem**, **An idea** or **A question** — write your message (up to 4000
characters) and press **Send**. For a problem, say what you did, what you expected and what
happened instead.

## What is attached

So a report can be acted on without asking you again, OSO attaches **diagnostic context**
automatically: the page you were on (anything in its address that may be a password, token or code
is replaced by `[redacted]`), the app version, your browser, the window size, your browser's language
and time zone, the theme, your computer's clock, the page's last few script errors, and — when you
are signed in — your account and the workspace you are in. **Show what is attached** in the dialog
shows the context exactly as it will be sent, apart from your account and the app version, which
the server adds.

## What happens next

Reports go to the instance's administrator, who reads them in
[Instance administration](admin.md#feedback). There is no reply in the app, and you cannot read a
report back after sending it. To keep the form from being misused, each account (or, when not signed
in, each network address) can send at most 5 reports in 10 minutes.
